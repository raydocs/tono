# Tono 0.0.63

- WeChat now stays on the direct path. Its main channel runs on port 80, and that port was being sent through the protected exit — so on a connection from China WeChat's traffic to Chinese carrier servers was making a round trip to the United States and back, which is the delay and the spinning users reported. Two audit logs from one account showed 82% and 72% of WeChat's connections leaving through the exit; both now go direct, with the exit still available as a fallback if the direct path fails.
- Adds a Network Log Upload setting, on during the test programme. While it is on, Tono uploads its own audit log — the hostnames you connected to, the process that opened each connection, and which rule and route it matched — to Tono support, so a routing fault can be diagnosed without asking you to find and send a file. Credentials, URL queries and page content are removed before anything is written to the log, and TLS bodies are never observed. Uploads are linked to your account, readable only by Tono administrators, and deleted after 14 days. Turning the setting off stops the upload and nothing from the log leaves your Mac.
- The Support page can send the newest log segment immediately, for when you are on a support conversation and waiting for the next upload is the difference between catching a live fault and losing it.
- Corrects the Aggregated App Routing Research setting, which described itself as on by default while actually shipping off.
