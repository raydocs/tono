`cleanup.rs` `retire_timed_out_generation` now records the retirement as automatic (`TonoInner::retire_timed_out_connection_generation`). `stale_after_arm` (via `stale_arm_release`) compensates a late arm of such a generation with `tono_release_kill_switch_applying_narrow`; explicit causes (Disconnect, sign-out, Restore, quit) keep the plain release, and a later explicit release still runs after the compensation because it needs the privileged writer the detached child's reader blocks.
