## 5. Native quality and brand consistency

**Keep the family identity, not identical platform chrome.** Both apps correctly use the newer sun/horizon mark rather than the old TO/purple identity or a generic shield. The common sea, dawn arrival, warm Connect/Retry emphasis, subdued Disconnect, warning/error semantics, exit capsule and route lanes are consistent. Decision 095 and the iOS brand generator / Android vector resources substantiate that provenance.

iOS earns native character from SwiftUI detents, swipe dismissal, zoom source transitions, system navigation material and the real Dynamic Island presentation. Android earns it from Material modal sheets/back navigation, the actual OS VPN permission dialog, Quick Settings tile and native notification rendering. An opaque content sheet is not itself a glass defect: it protects legibility and avoids glass-on-glass layering.

The differences that matter are practical: iOS's cloud-error recovery action is not wired through; Android's Settings entry remains high; both can make the ordinary Home material more perceptible without adding decoration. Neither needs a wholesale visual restart.
