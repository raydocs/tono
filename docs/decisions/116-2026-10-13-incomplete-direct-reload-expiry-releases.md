## 2026-10-13 · When a DIRECT reload that never committed (Bracket or Pending) expires and nobody recovers the session, does a non-strict machine stay Blocked?

- Status: provisional
- Chosen: no, after a bounded grace. The expiry still narrows to exact Blocked at once (unchanged: an App that is alive and mid-reload keeps the fail-closed behavior of #777/#926). A non-strict session is then queued for the existing epoch-fenced retirement: if no fresh Connect (re-arm) or verification clears it within 90 s (the Pending lease length), the Service stops the Core and opens general traffic with the AI hold, exactly like a lost committed heartbeat. A strict kill switch never queues; Lock and MarkVerified stay admitted during the grace, unlike committed expiry, because the App that owns an in-flight reload may still be alive. Rejected: releasing at the expiry instant (cuts a live reload; #777/#926 deliberately excluded incomplete phases), and the status quo (Blocked is reported healthy, the non-strict unhealthy-release path is never reached, and with no App left nothing ever opens the network).
- Why stricter: protection is never released while the App can still recover the session, the AI hold is installed before general traffic opens (decision 031), and strict mode is untouched. Decision 036 (owner) ranks the user always having a network above holding a general block; this only bounds how long an abandoned Blocked state may last. The cost, accepted here, is that a live App whose recovery takes longer than 90 s after an incomplete-reload expiry loses its Core and has to Connect again.
- Recovery inside the grace: a live replacement lease (the App finished a new reload, which commits and renews without another MarkVerified) withdraws the queued release at the next watchdog tick and is also checked at retirement time (`incomplete_recovery_superseded`), so the old deadline never retires a session the App just recovered. When only the Blocked intent write failed but the exact Blocked WFP was proved, the release is still queued; a failed narrowing never queues. The window the incomplete reload replaces (a longer fresh-Connect verification window) is remembered and restored on withdrawal, never cleared; an earlier existing window is left alone. Remaining race: a reload that commits between the retirement check and the Core stop loses that Core and has to Connect again.
- Applied in: `queue_incomplete_direct_recovery_release` (`apps/windows/service/src/core/windows_kill_switch/state.rs`), called from `reconcile_direct_watchdog_invalidation_unlocked`; finding R3KS1-PENDING-EXPIRY, issue #1056.
