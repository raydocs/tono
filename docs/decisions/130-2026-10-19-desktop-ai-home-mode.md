## 2026-10-19 · What does the desktop AI Home mode protect, and what does it leave open?

- Status: owner-decided direction, 2026-10-19: “主要保证 ai 走家宽 ip 和 dns 不泄露假如家宽断了连不上那就断 和 proton 的 killer 模式一样 就行 其他走直连的就可以放开 udp 这些了” and “海外名单 其余直连 … 就收录的那些就好”. Implementation sequence (4 PRs) accepted with “开始做”. The default flip waits for installed-device testing.
- Chosen: a third desktop graph, AI Home. AI/named Home group → cloud → assigned Home, with Home DNS through the same path, exactly as three-tier (same matchers, same Home outbound and DNS modulo tags). Recognized AI UDP/ICMP is refused, never sent DIRECT. Listed video exceptions and listed overseas hosts → cloud (their UDP refused). Everything else, including UDP/QUIC, LAN and unlisted hosts → DIRECT. No China IP table and no automatic geography classification. Rejected: strict three-tier as the default (fails closed for all traffic on quit/failure and refuses ordinary UDP), and a China-IP split (slower and outside the owner's list-only choice).
- Failure behavior: AI stays fail-closed (Home or cloud down → AI cannot connect; it never falls back to DIRECT). Quit, Core death or helper failure must not lock ordinary traffic. The helper binds the mode non-strict (no strict intent recorded), and an AI Home graph cannot replace a strict three-tier session. The AI-scoped PF hold that keeps AI blocked after the Core is gone is PR2; until then this graph is not offered in the app.
- Coverage boundary: as in [128](128-2026-10-17-desktop-three-tier-routing-boundaries.md), only listed AI domains and the listed Anthropic CIDRs are recognized. AI reached through DoH, ECH, a shared CDN or a raw unlisted IP is ordinary traffic and goes DIRECT. The Home line rides the cloud tunnel. Stable and three-tier graphs are unchanged.
- Applied in: PR1 `feat/ai-home-contract` (graph contract, generator, helper admission and binding; helper 4.52.59). Desktop macOS only; no candidate or publication.
