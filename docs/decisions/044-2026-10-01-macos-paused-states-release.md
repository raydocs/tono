## 2026-10-01 · May a macOS pause that waits for the user keep PF armed with the Core stopped?

- Status: provisional
- Chosen: no. Without a strict kill switch (macOS stores none), the HY2 three-strike pause, the hold after an unexpected restart and a wake that finds either of those pauses take the automatic release (`releaseAfterFailure`, AI hold kept) at once. The UI shows "restoring normal internet" until the release settles open, then "back on normal internet". Rejected: keeping the PF hold and adding a 30 s idle poll (Windows R2-F2 style), because none of these states re-arms within a bounded few seconds; the helper's core-down watchdog already lifted the hold about 30 s later. A helper that rejects this app cannot be released or observed without the administrator prompt, so that pause is unchanged and tracked in [#1305](https://github.com/raydocs/tono/issues/1305).
- Why stricter: no new traffic is allowed beyond what the watchdog already allowed 30 s later. AI services stay blocked by the same selective release. Nothing reconnects automatically: the restart hold and the HY2 refusal still wait for the user. A failed release keeps "Kill Switch remains active".
- Applied in: [#1287](https://github.com/raydocs/tono/issues/1287) fix PR; follows [decision 031](031-2026-09-30-fail-open-keeps-ai-block.md) and #1285.
