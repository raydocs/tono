## 2026-10-04 · A device whose exit identity is not acknowledged yet: serve a fresh shared UUID, or answer 503 and retry soon?

- Status: provisional
- Chosen: 503 `EXIT_IDENTITY_PROPAGATING`, and the clients retry soon. The control plane serves the account's shared credential to a device only when that credential already exists, is not retired, and every exit served in this response has acknowledged a roster after it was created. Otherwise it answers 503. It never mints a shared credential on the device catalog path. Windows asks again 15 s after a sync that ended on this reason, from the same periodic task (other failures keep the 300 s period). macOS, with no cached catalog at launch, stays in its non-ready state and asks again every 15 s, at most 20 times (about five minutes), then fails as before and offers Retry. Rejected: keep minting and serving an unacknowledged shared UUID (the exit rejects it, so the first connect fails); fall back to any existing shared credential without the readiness check (same failure for one the exits have not installed); unbounded macOS wait (a dead exit would leave the launch spinning with no Retry button); a second periodic loop on Windows.
- Why stricter: no identity reaches a client before the exits that will check it hold it, and no new shared credential is created for a device. Retired and device_only accounts keep their no-fallback rule. Nothing changes PF/WFP, protection release or decisions 030/031: the macOS wait holds no lock, cleanup cancels it, and a read retired by an account or state change is never consumed. Cost: a new account that used to receive a minted UUID at once now waits for the exits' next roster round (about a minute) before its first catalog; while exits are being provisioned with no acknowledgement, catalogs answer 503 instead of serving the shared UUID.
